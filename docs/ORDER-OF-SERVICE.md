# Order of service and timing

Researched Nov 9, 2026. Sources are listed at the bottom.

## The fixed point: 11:00

Everything is timed from the two minutes of silence. **The Last Post's final note begins the
silence at 11:00** (VAC: "the _Last Post_ is finished at exactly 11 am, marking the beginning of
the two minutes of silent reflection"). The Last Post, silence and Rouse "take place one after the
other, without interruption", so the silence runs 11:00–11:02.

With the official CAF recording (1:24), the Last Post starts at **10:58:36**. The app shows this time
and can start it by itself ("Start it automatically").

## Recommended order (Royal Canadian Legion)

| # | Item | Audio | When |
|---|------|-------|------|
| 1 | Land acknowledgment | — | before 11:00 |
| 2 | Opening remarks | — | |
| 3 | **O Canada** | 1:36 | e.g. ~10:56, finishing by 10:58:30 |
| 4 | **Last Post** | 1:24 | **10:58:36 → 11:00:00** |
| 5 | **Two minutes of silence** | 2:00 | **11:00:00 → 11:02:00** |
| 6 | Lament (piper, optional) | — | |
| 7 | **Rouse** | 0:23 | ~11:02 |
| 8 | Act of Remembrance / Commitment to Remember | — | |
| 9 | Prayers | — | |
| 10 | Placing of wreaths (often with *In Flanders Fields* read) | — | |
| 11 | Benediction / guest speakers | — | |
| 12 | **God Save the King** | 0:49 | closes the ceremony |

### Working backwards for a typical 11:00 service

- 10:50  Gathering, parade in place
- 10:53  Land acknowledgment, opening remarks
- 10:56  O Canada (ends ~10:57:40, leaving time to settle)
- 10:58:36  Last Post
- 11:00  Two minutes of silence
- 11:02  (Lament) → Rouse
- 11:03  Act of Remembrance, prayers, wreaths, speakers
- ~11:30  God Save the King, dismissal

## Notes from the guidance

- **O Canada** comes after the opening remarks in the Legion's order, and before them (just after
  dignitaries arrive) in VAC's suggested sequence. Either way it's well before the Last Post.
- **No music during the silence**: CAF says no instruments at all, bagpipes included.
- **Lament placement** varies. CAF allows it before the Last Post, after the Rouse, or (at the
  parade commander's discretion) after the silence and before the Rouse. Legion ceremonies usually
  put it after the silence, before the Rouse. National ceremonies use *Flowers of the Forest*. The
  app does not include a Lament; if you need one, the CAF's recording is linked below and any row's
  right-click menu can swap in another file.
- **21-gun salute**, if any, starts immediately after the silence, with the Rouse or Lament.
- **God Save the King:** VAC places it "prior to the departure of the dignitaries and the marching
  out of the Colour Party and military parade". Canadian Heritage notes that outside royal occasions "it is no longer
  customary to play the Royal Anthem at public events in Canada", but the Legion's order of service
  still closes with it. It's your call.
- If the service isn't at 11:00 (schools, Nov 10 events), change "Silence at" in the app and the
  Last Post start time moves with it.
